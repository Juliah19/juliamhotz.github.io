Values I hold

Healthcare design is a moral responsibility

When users are scared, in pain, or lost in a system they don't understand, a bad interaction has real consequences. I carry that weight into every decision.

Words are design decisions

The button label, the error message, the empty state — they're as consequential as any layout choice. I write everything I ship.

Design and engineering should share the problem

The best work I've been part of happened when design and engineering co-owned the challenge from the start — not when I handed over a spec.

Equity isn't a feature — it's the baseline

In healthcare, who gets access to a good experience determines who gets access to care. Accessible, inclusive design isn't a stretch goal. It's the job.
